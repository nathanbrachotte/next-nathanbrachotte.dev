Soulether is a membership for people who want astrology that is actually about them. Every reading is worked out from your own birth chart and where the planets are today, not your star sign.

## Features

- **Daily check-in**: a horoscope in your inbox every morning, explaining why the day reads the way it does
- **The week ahead**: a longer reading every Sunday, built from the moon phase and your own transits, with the days that matter called out
- **Tarot**: draw one card a day, and a full five-card spread once a week. Every draw is kept with its question
- **Your people**: read anyone you care about against your own chart

## Technical Stack

- Next.js with Supabase for data and Auth.js for sign-in
- Stripe subscriptions, starting with a $1 trial
- Personalized daily emails built with React Email and sent through Resend
- Paid acquisition funnels tracked end to end with PostHog
