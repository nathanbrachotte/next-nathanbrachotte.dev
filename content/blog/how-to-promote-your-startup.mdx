You shipped. Now nobody knows it exists.

The cheapest fix is to list the product everywhere people look for new tools. Each listing brings a bit of traffic, some bring a backlink, and together they tell Google your domain is real. None of it replaces talking to users, but it's an afternoon of work that keeps paying.

I went through a long list of these sites and checked every link. About one in four was dead: parked domains, broken SSL certificates, directories that disappeared. What's left is below.

A few things I learned along the way:

- **Almost everything needs an account.** Set aside time to sign up, verify emails and fill in profiles. Keep your logo, screenshots, a one-liner and a longer description ready to paste.
- **Not every listing gives you a backlink.** Some only link to you on a paid tier. Check before you spend time on it.
- **Badges are the usual price of a free listing.** Several sites will list you for free if you put their badge in your footer.
- **Free queues are slow.** Expect to wait weeks unless you pay to skip.
- **Launch days need prep.** Product Hunt and Show HN reward a planned launch with people ready to try the product and comment. A drive-by submit wastes your one shot.
